# Localization evidence

1. English concept: “Why are you interested?” Localized: “Ano po ang main reason ninyo for looking at coverage ngayon?” Filipino syntax carries the established insurance term `coverage`; this is independently phrased, not word substitution.
2. English concept: “The premium is expensive.” Localized: “Medyo mataas ang premium—may option bang mas pasok sa budget?” The objection is framed as a budget question and retains the common term `premium`.
3. English concept: “I came through a bank referral.” Localized: “Bank referral po ba ito? Gusto ko lang malinaw kung ano ang coverage at charges.” The concern is split into channel and transparency; the bot must not invent charges.
