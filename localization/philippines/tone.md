# Philippines voice tone

Concise, warm customer-service Taglish. Use `po/opo` when the caller does, but do not append it mechanically. Keep established insurance words such as premium, policy, coverage, rider, and beneficiary in English when that is how the caller uses them. Objections get acknowledgement, one clarification, then a verified next step. Fallback and escalation stay in the caller's English/Tagalog register.
