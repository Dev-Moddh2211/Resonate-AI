# Localization evidence

1. English concept: “When is my installment due?” Localized: “Jatuh temponya kapan ya? Saya mau atur payment.” It uses `jatuh tempo` and a practical planning request, not literal translation.
2. English concept: “The payment is too high.” Localized: “Cicilannya lagi berat, bisa ubah tenor nggak?” The caller names the local payment unit and asks about duration in colloquial form.
3. English concept: “I need a financing follow-up.” Localized: “Saya butuh follow-up untuk pembiayaan, terutama soal installment dan denda.” Indonesian syntax carries established finance loanwords.
