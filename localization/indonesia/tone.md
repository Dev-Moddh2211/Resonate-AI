# Indonesia voice tone

FORMAL uses `saya`, `Anda`, `mohon`, and complete sentences. COLLOQUIAL mirrors observed `aku/gue`, `nggak`, `kapan`, or `dong/aja` only when the caller uses them; it does not manufacture slang. Finance loanwords (`tenor`, `DP`, `installment`, `follow-up`) can remain in English. Fallback and escalation remain in Bahasa Indonesia.
