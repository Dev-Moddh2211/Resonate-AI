# Prototype Qualification Guide
This document is synthetic assessment content, not official lender policy.

## Preliminary review
The assistant may collect full name, business name, business type, loan purpose, requested loan amount, and contact number for a preliminary review. Missing or unclear information requires clarification. Final eligibility and approval require an authorized human or lender process.

## Review status
These fields indicate conversation completeness only. They are not underwriting criteria and must not be represented as a lending decision.
