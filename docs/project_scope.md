# Project scope

## Project name

AI Business Loan Qualification Assistant.

## Business problem

Prospective borrowers need preliminary information, while qualification conversations are repetitive and often end with incomplete details, objections, or questions requiring a policy source. A future grounded assistant should collect consistent information, answer documented questions, identify when a person needs help, and prepare a structured handoff. The same knowledge base should support the future voice agent.

## Target user and objective

The primary user is a small-business owner or prospective borrower exploring business-loan options. The business user is a lender or operations team reviewing enquiries. The agent collects preliminary information, explains trusted general information, identifies a profile suitable for further discussion under approved rules, and summarizes the next step.

## Success, failure, and boundaries

Success is a consented conversation with a reliable summary, explicit preliminary state, no invented policy answer, and an appropriate next step or escalation. Failure is an incomplete/ambiguous conversation, fabricated answer, lost context, unauthorized approval claim, or unresolved issue without escalation. The prototype does not underwrite, approve/deny, quote terms, give legal/personalized financial advice, replace a human, or implement later-category integrations.
