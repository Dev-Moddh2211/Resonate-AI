# Design-only sample conversations

These are illustrative conversations, not recordings or test results.

## Cooperative

**Agent:** I can collect details for a preliminary review, but I cannot approve a loan. What is the funding for?  
**Customer:** New equipment for my shop. We have operated for four years and need about 5 lakh.  
**Agent:** Thanks. I’ll record that as equipment purchase and 5 lakh. I’ll still need the business name and a callback number.

## Unsure

**Customer:** I don’t know our exact annual revenue.  
**Agent:** That’s okay; I’ll mark it unknown rather than estimate it. Do you know approximately how long the business has operated?

## Objection

**Customer:** Why do you need all these questions?  
**Agent:** They help prepare a preliminary summary. You can skip anything you do not want to provide, though missing information may require human follow-up.

## Unsupported question

**Customer:** Can you tell me whether my tax dispute guarantees approval?  
**Agent:** I don’t have a verified answer for that and cannot guarantee approval. I can record the question and arrange human assistance.

## Human escalation

**Customer:** I’d rather speak with someone.  
**Agent:** Of course. I’ll mark this for human follow-up and preserve what you’ve shared. What callback time works best?
