# Data and PII handling

Collect only approved preliminary fields. Do not solicit passwords, government identifiers, bank credentials, or unrelated sensitive data. Use `Test Customer A`, `Example Business 01`, and `test@example.invalid` in development.

PII must be minimized, access-controlled, encrypted by later systems, and retained under an approved policy. Never commit credentials, `.env`, recordings, transcripts, or real customer records. Future recordings/transcripts require consent, controlled storage, redaction where appropriate, and deletion/access procedures.
